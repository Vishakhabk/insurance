# Golden Repository Review Checklist

## Business completeness
- [ ] Product and insurance line are defined
- [ ] Jurisdiction and regulator are identified
- [ ] Personas and responsibilities are defined
- [ ] End-to-end lifecycle is covered
- [ ] In-scope and out-of-scope items are explicit

## Functional completeness
- [ ] Main, alternate, exception, and reversal flows exist
- [ ] Status model and transitions are documented
- [ ] Rules are atomic and versioned
- [ ] Documents and notifications are covered
- [ ] Integration failure handling is defined

## Technical completeness
- [ ] APIs and events are identified
- [ ] Data ownership and retention are defined
- [ ] Authorization matrix is included
- [ ] Audit and observability requirements exist
- [ ] Performance, availability, and recovery targets are stated

## AI-generated artifact quality
- [ ] Output is grounded in the selected source scope
- [ ] No unrelated epics or invented requirements are introduced
- [ ] Every story has testable acceptance criteria
- [ ] Traceability is preserved from BRD to tests
- [ ] Assumptions and open questions are separated from confirmed facts
