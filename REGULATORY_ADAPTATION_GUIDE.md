# Regulatory Adaptation Guide

Do not treat this repository as a complete legal or regulatory baseline.

Before implementation, identify:
- Country and state/province
- Applicable insurance regulator
- Insurance line and product category
- Product filing and policy wording requirements
- KYC/AML and sanctions obligations
- Data protection and cross-border transfer requirements
- Consumer protection and complaint handling
- Electronic signature and document validity
- Tax, accounting, and financial reporting rules
- Record retention and audit requirements
- Accessibility and language obligations

For India-specific implementations, consult current IRDAI regulations, circulars, guidelines, and applicable privacy and financial requirements. The IRDAI describes insurance repositories as systems for maintaining insurance policies electronically and supporting policy servicing; applicability depends on the actual solution scope.
