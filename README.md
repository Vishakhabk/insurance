# Insurance Project Golden Repository

A reusable reference repository for generating high-quality insurance BRDs, epics, features, user stories, acceptance criteria, UX prompts, API specifications, data models, and test cases.

## Scope
Supports life, health, motor, property, travel, and commercial insurance workflows. Adapt the regulatory and product rules to the target country, insurance line, and operating model.

## Core lifecycle
1. Customer and party management
2. Product and plan configuration
3. Quote and illustration
4. Proposal/application
5. KYC, AML, underwriting, and risk assessment
6. Policy issuance
7. Premium billing and payment
8. Endorsement and policy servicing
9. Claims FNOL, assessment, adjudication, and settlement
10. Renewal, cancellation, lapse, reinstatement, and reporting

## Important
This is a generic accelerator, not legal advice or a certified regulatory implementation. Validate requirements against the applicable regulator, policy wording, internal controls, and product filings.
