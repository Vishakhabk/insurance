# Business Rules Catalog

Rules should be uniquely identified, versioned, effective-dated, and linked to products and jurisdictions.

Examples:
- BR-001: A policy cannot be issued unless mandatory proposal validations pass.
- BR-002: Premium calculations must use the configured product version effective on the quote date.
- BR-003: A payment callback must be idempotent.
- BR-004: A claim must be checked against policy status and coverage dates.
- BR-005: Approval thresholds must enforce delegated authority.
- BR-006: Changes to regulated or sensitive customer data require authorization and audit logging.
- BR-007: A user may access only policies and claims permitted by their role and organizational scope.
- BR-008: Policy documents must show the applicable version and effective date.
